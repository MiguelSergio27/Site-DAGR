// Member handbook

Standards
& conduct

What we expect from every member of D.A.G.R. — and what you can expect from us.

18+ · Microphone · PC preferred, console OK · 2–3 days a week

// Standards

01
Know your role

Declare a primary and a secondary. Become proficient with both.

02
Follow the chain

Orders come from your team leader. Leaders answer to Command.

03
Keep comms clean

Short, clear, on the net. Say what matters and get off.

04
Show up ready

Aim for 2 to 3 days a week. Share your availability and timezone.

05
One company

Your team serves the fight. Remember the mission.

06
Earn your tier

FNG, Regular, Veteran, Leader. Promotions are earned through action.

// Code of conduct

01
Respect everyone

No harassment, hate or slurs. Disagree without the drama.

02
Play clean

No cheats, exploits or griefing. Ever.

03
Mission first on comms

Keep the net clear. Save banter for downtime.

04
Take issues to leaders

Tell your team leader. About a leader? Contact GROWBIGGER or SWEEP.

05
Be honest

Give real availability and real quals. Own your mistakes.

06
Help the new guys

Everyone was an FNG once. Teach, do not gatekeep.

Respect is not optional.
